# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 24 September 2026

Froze the final manuscript figure layout script and all Figure 1, Figure 2, and supplementary figure source tables in the repository, with the rendered exports in the checksummed release attachment. Full offline reproduction now regenerates and checks the current manuscript figure CSVs in addition to the original numerical analyses and earlier figure sources. The model-response evidence archive is unchanged from v1.0.0. No new model requests, cases, or statistical tests were added. The manuscript itself is not in the repository.

## 1.0.0 — 23 September 2026

Initial offline reproducibility release. Includes three formal decision rounds, format/coding analyses, historical matching and controlled service measurements. No additional model queries or inferential tests were introduced for this release. Local evidence paths were rebased; raw response strings were preserved. The acquisition ledger is replaced with public JSONL plus a disposable compatibility index. Manuscript drafts and author declarations are not included.
